Security & privacy

Your data is yours. We built it that way.

Reachout handles your inbox, your contacts and your job hunt, so security isn't an add-on.

Encrypted, all of it

Contacts, emails, tokens and files are encrypted before they're stored. Email addresses are looked up by a keyed fingerprint, never in plain text.

No passwords to steal

You sign in with a one-time code sent to your email. Sessions are short-lived, and you can log out on every device at once.

Your accounts, your voice

Messages go out from your own Gmail, Outlook and WhatsApp. Your mailbox is read read-only, and nothing is ever marked as read.

Respectful by design

No LinkedIn scraping or bots, and no guessed email addresses. Research uses only emails that companies publish themselves.

Hardened and rate-limited

Strict security headers on every page, limits on sign-in attempts and sending, and a daily cap that protects your sender reputation.

Leave any time

Deleting your account removes everything: contacts, emails, files, website and history. No lingering copies in the app.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Ask us. We usually reply within a working day.

Whose number and email are messages sent from?

Your own. You connect Gmail or Outlook with an app password and link WhatsApp by scanning a QR code, just like WhatsApp Web. Recipients see your name, your address and your number.

How does Reachout know who replied?

It reads your mailbox read-only and matches replies to the emails you sent, even when people answer in the same thread. It shows what they wrote, what they're asking for, and suggests an answer you can send from inside the app.

How are my job applications tracked?

Reachout reads application emails from job sites and companies (applied, in review, assessment, interview, offer, rejected) and keeps one timeline per application. It syncs every morning, and you can edit, hide or merge anything.

Can I build a website without coding?

Yes. Pick a theme, fonts and colours, add sections like projects, experience and contact, and publish. Your projects can come straight from GitHub with their live links, and messages from the contact form land in your Leads page.

Is it safe for my WhatsApp account?

WhatsApp doesn't officially allow automated messaging, and numbers that message many strangers can be restricted. Reachout spaces messages out and caps daily volume, but keep batches small and contact people who expect to hear from you.

Does Reachout scrape LinkedIn or guess email addresses?

No. LinkedIn is used only through its official sign-in and sharing, with no scraping, auto-messaging or auto-applying. Reachout never guesses people's email addresses.

Who can see my data?

Only you. Every account has a private workspace, everything stored is encrypted, sign-in uses one-time codes instead of passwords, and deleting your account removes all of your data.

What does it cost?

Reachout is free to use. There's no credit card and nothing to install.
